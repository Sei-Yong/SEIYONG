Interface Residue Analyzer

Identify antigen and antibody residues that lose solvent-accessible surface area upon complex formation.

Interface residues by chain

Calculation and selection criteria

Only the selected chains enter the calculation. The antigen is one partner. The selected antibody chains together form the other. The isolated partners retain their bound coordinates.

Residue ΔSASA = isolated-partner SASA − complex SASA, summed over the residue’s atoms. The default selects residues with ΔSASA ≥ the cutoff. The alternative follows the atom threshold in Jason Vertrees’ PyMOLWiki InterfaceResidues script: a residue qualifies when any atom has |ΔSASA| ≥ the cutoff. Both modes report the residue total and largest absolute atomic change. A zero cutoff includes all residues.

Areas use a Lee–Richards calculation with 20 slices, ProtOr radii and a 1.4 Å probe. Values can differ from PyMOL’s dot-sampled areas. The first model’s ATOM records are used; hydrogens, HETATM records and unselected chains are excluded. Labels follow structure numbering, including insertion codes.
