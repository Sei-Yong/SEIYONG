How contacts are assigned

All residues with Cα on the selected partners are screened independently of the His screen, its Cα cutoff and loop ranges. For each residue pair and interaction type, the table reports the closest qualifying contact for each antigen/antibody group combination. Backbone atoms are N, CA, C, O and OXT. Aromatic rings belong to the side chain.

Hydrophobic: side-chain C/S atoms of A/V/I/L/M/F/W/P/Y within 4.0 Å. Hydrogen bond candidate: compatible backbone or side-chain donor and acceptor heavy atoms within 3.5 Å; hydrogen positions, directionality and protonation are unresolved. Salt bridge: Lys/Arg side-chain nitrogen and Asp/Glu carboxylate oxygen within 4.0 Å. One pair may qualify for multiple types.

π–π: complete Phe, Tyr, Trp or His rings, centroids within 5.5 Å, acute interplanar angle ≤30° or ≥60°, and smaller lateral offset ≤2 Å. Ring distances are centroid distances. Static coordinates support geometric hypotheses; side-chain placement in a model can change assignments.

How the His screening score is calculated

Binder residues within the distance cutoff are ranked after wild-type His is excluded. Higher scores indicate higher screening priority.

Base score　24 + 2.1 × max(0, 10 − d), where d is the shortest distance in Å from the binder Cα to an antigen atom.

Antigen environment　Add 1.06 × max(P, H) + 0.25 × min(P, H) − N. Each term uses the distance from the binder Cα to the nearest eligible antigen atom. A term is zero if the atom is absent or outside its cutoff.

  • P · Lys/Arg terminal nitrogen, p < 9 Å. P = max(0, 18 − 2.3 × (p − 3)).
  • H · Side-chain carbon of A/V/I/L/M/F/W/P/Y, h < 9 Å. H = max(0, 17 − 2.2 × (h − 3)).
  • N · Asp/Glu side-chain carboxyl oxygen, n < 8 Å. N = max(0, 17 − 2.4 × (n − 3)).

Orientation　Compare the Cα→Cβ vector with the direction to the nearest antigen atom. Add 6 if the cosine exceeds 0.2, subtract 7 below −0.3, and use zero otherwise or if the direction is unavailable.

Residue adjustments　Subtract 18 for binder G/P, 12 for D/E/K/R, or 5 for F/Y/W. Subtract another 3 if an antigen F/Y/W aromatic carbon is less than 5.5 Å from the binder Cα.

The total is rounded and limited to 0–100. This heuristic ranks screening candidates. It does not estimate pH-specific affinity or the probability of a successful substitution.
