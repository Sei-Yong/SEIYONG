Calculation method

Below 14 bases: Tm = 2(A + T) + 4(G + C). From 14 bases: Tm = 64.9 + 41(G + C − 16.4) / length. Letters in the formulas represent base counts.

For mixed bases, the temperature range uses the lowest and highest possible GC counts. Mean GC content assumes equal proportions of the allowed bases at each position.
