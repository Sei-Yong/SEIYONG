Residue SASA Calculator

Calculate solvent-accessible surface area (SASA) for each residue and its side chain from a PDB or mmCIF structure.

Surface-exposed mutation candidates

Positions are ranked by relative SASA, with side-chain SASA used to break ties. Qualifying Cys, Gly and Pro residues, incomplete residues and residues with zero side-chain SASA appear under “Positions for review”.

Calculation method and interpretation

All chains are analyzed together. The Lee–Richards calculation uses 20 slices per atom, ProtOr atomic radii and a probe radius of 1.4 Å.

Total SASA is the sum of the solvent-accessible areas of all atoms in a residue. Relative SASA is total SASA divided by the reference maximum for that residue type, multiplied by 100. Values can exceed 100%. N/A indicates that the residue has no reference value.

Side-chain SASA is the accessible area of atoms other than N, CA, C, O and OXT. Side-chain fraction is the percentage of total SASA contributed by the side chain. If total SASA is zero, the fraction is shown as N/A.

For mmCIF files, the calculator uses author chain and residue identifiers when available and falls back to label identifiers. It reads ATOM records from the first model and excludes hydrogen, deuterium and HETATM records. For each residue with alternate conformations, it uses the first conformation encountered along with shared atoms. If an atom name has no assigned radius, the calculator reports its location.
